What we're building

A club for anyone who wants a career in law and wants to stop guessing how to get one.

The path into law school and legal careers isn't taught in any one class — it's passed down, often informally, and often not to everyone equally. We exist to close that gap.

Founded by students who wanted better answers than they were getting, Women in Law Society gives Georgetown undergraduates direct access to the people and information that usually take years to find on your own.

  • 01
    MentorshipPaired guidance from law students and practicing attorneys, from first-year through application season.
  • 02
    Skill-buildingLSAT strategy sessions, mock cold calls, and workshops on writing a personal statement that isn't generic.
  • 03
    AdvocacySpeaker events and panels on the barriers women still face in the legal profession — and what's changing.
